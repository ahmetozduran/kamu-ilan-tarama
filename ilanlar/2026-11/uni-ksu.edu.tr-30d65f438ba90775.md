Kahramanmaraş Sütçü İmam University

                                  Erasmus Policy Statement

        Kahramanmaraş Sütçü İmam University aims to reach one of the top leading
universities in the world in terms of teaching and research with the help of forming and
developing its internationalisation strategies on the basis of cooperation with national and
international institutions. With this respect, international and European partnerships and
collaboration keeps an important place in development of our university. As a university
taking part in Erasmus+ project for 16 years, KSU regularly promote exchange several
students and staff to/from several European countries every year. Additionally, our university
takes part in national and international education fairs in order to strengthen and develop new
and already existing network of our institution. While choosing its partners, KSU takes
several criteria into consideration such as common research and teaching interests, academic
performance, technical quality and language ability.
        Our institution guarantees to respect the equality and transparency criteria set out by
the programme for selection of the participants of the projects. Therefore, there will be equal
opportunity supplied by our university to participants coming from all kind of candidates
without regarding gender, ethnicity, race, religion, nationality, social or economical
backgrounds. Taking the Erasmus User’s Guide rules into consideration, participants with
disabilities are also promoted with additional points during selection process.
        Erasmus exchange program is one of the most important parts of our university’s
internationalisation strategies as it enhances academic and professional development of the
students and staff through mobility activities. Additionally, it promotes their career and helps
their employment. Within this respect, KSU makes several attempts to increase the visibility
of the programme in our city, region, country and in international platforms. Erasmus+
program is promoted at KSU through several ways. At regular intervals, Erasmus Office hold
information meetings about the program for students, staff and departmental coordinators. All
the information and developments about the program are announced at our website and other
types of information sharing such as brochures, e-mails, announcements, meetings and
activities are implemented.
        KSU is determined to follow the principles of Erasmus User’s Guide rules, taking the
direction towards modern internal management and active promotion of the university, as
well as increasing participation in international programmes and scientific initiatives of EU
countries and fulfill and create international educational and scientific programmes. This
strategic aim of the university conforms to the activities within the framework of Erasmus+
programme. These activities have supported the priority goals of the university for the years
2021-2027 aiming at greater internationalisation and have led to obtaining a European profile.
Considering the above, the university still intends to make use of the innovative potential of
the Erasmus programme and pursue the following priority goals:
              extending our cooperation scope with scientific centres at international level in
               order to make available the body of scientific work of other countries for our
               students and staff and allowing them to increase their qualifications,
               knowledge and experience which are essential elements of a successful career,
               both at university and in the workplace.
              KSU will support the mobility initiatives of staff and students of all three study
               cycles to the institutions abroad through making use of the experience of
               international exchange programmes and appreciating fully the importance of
               the period of study/traineeship/practice realized abroad.
              KSU contemplates to extend the existent offered courses in foreign language
               and to develop already existing integrated curricula in order to execute the
               balance in exchange and to increase the mobility activities.
              KSU aims to increase the competitiveness of the university, students and staff
               in Turkish an international marketplace through Erasmus+ actions. Those
               actions will also put an effect on performance of our university and the cultural
               and economic development of our region and country by allowing the
               participants to use their full capacity of scientific potential and therefore it will
               increase the quality of education.
        International research, lectures and publications abroad, as well as the ability to work
in a multicultural environment are important criteria at KSU. The university also has several
staff who have academic experience and scientific degrees at international universities from
all over the world and we aim to increase the number of those staff. The most important aim
in staff mobility exchange is to disseminate the academic achievement, to develop the
education system through cooperation to develop common educational and research project
and to benefit from knowledge and experience of the incoming staff. Another important aim
of our university is to strenghten the quality of administrative issues through administrative
staff mobility. Therefore, KSU will contribute to their adjustment skills for working in an
international environment which meets flexibility, international and integration demands of
the universtiy in terms of all educational/scientific/administrative activities undertaken in
international area along with the regional and global demands. For choosing the partners,
effectiveness and quality factors in particular academic disciplines and developmental
possibilities within Erasmus+ programme's key actions will be taken into consideration.
Additionally, our research profile will be another criteria for developing cooperation with
universities. Besides, KSU aims to reinforce actions which males access of bussiness people
into scientific database and university’s research outcomes possible. KSU constantly expands
its offer of foreign language courses in order to create an integrated curriculum of courses in
foreign languages for extending its internationalization. Those actions will result in develoing
double, multiple and joint degrees. Within this respect, KSU follows and takes part in
preparatory visits, contact seminars and international meetings with an attempt to gain
partners and candidates to study in the double, multiple and joint degree system.

        KSU designates to support the organization and implementation of international
cooperation projects in teaching and training within the scope of the programme. Within this
respect, it is planned to several actions as follows:

      The most important priority of our institution will be to introduce innovational
       education solutions such as modern distance learning opportunuties which is gaining
       importance in global education each passing day, an advanced and effective
       technology platform for Erasmus without paper and to use modern information
       technologies that prepare the youth to enter the demanding job market.
      KSU will promote international cooperation projects in teaching and training in
       medicine, humanities, science and engineering in order to raise diversification and
       practical verification of the teaching and training offer.
      KSU will also develop and adopt a new system for concluding agreements in online
       platform in order to ensure transparency of the roles of the parties and conditions of
       financing. Therefore, these enterprises will promote student and staff mobility.
      Our university’s existing system for disseminating information on projects available
       within the new programme period will also be enhanced. Within this respect,
       International Relations Office of our university will share regular information about
       available projects, will support departments during application and realization of the
       project and supports departmental research and international cooperation delegates
       who are responsible for management of the project and dissemination of its effects.
      Appropriate university authorities and subordinate units will be responsible for
       implementation and organization of international projects in order to evaluate and
       measure whether outcomes are sustainable and balanced. There will be an active
       digital platform for adversiting results and effects of respective projects.

         The envisaged impact of our participation in the Erasmus+ Programme on our
institution can be summarized as follows:

      As KSU is a higher education institution, supporting and promoting research and
       educational methods which would join the students and scientists' efforts leading to
       important scientific discoveries is one of the most important target of our university.
       All kind of beneficial interdisciplinary and international scientific research will be
       promoted by our institution. One of our most important priorities will be to establish
       scientific and cultural connection in international platform. Our staff and students will
       be provided with the opportunuties to provide them with the skills required and needed
       in global area.
      As quality of the staff and international reputation of the university has an important
       effect on the education system and qualification of our graduates, taking part in
       Erasmus+ program will enhance the quality of our education system which will result
       in skilled and talented graduates who can meet and adjust to the international demands
       in workplace. Through mobility actions, we will have the possibility to observe,
       modify and adopt our curricula with respect to dynamically changing education
       systems.
      Erasmus+ programme will also have an undeniable positive effect on teaching quality
       and reinforce international research and teaching. Staff mobility and gained
       experiences will result in teaching students to be open to new ideas, good at creative
       problem solving and effective in team working. Such experiences will promote life
       long learning which will promote graduates to be more successful in marketplace.
   KSU aims to keep and develop already existing science and business contacts in order
    to comply with the needs of employers while designing curricula. In this respect, KSU
    will share and commercialize the research or development outcomes for improvement
    of enterprises.
   In order to simplify administrative work and facilitate quality control, KSU will
    enhance its management and financial policy which will combine quality and
    efficiency of administrative works supporting scientific, publishing and teaching
    activities.

       Bu aydınlatma metni, 7 Nisan 2016 tarihli ve 29677 sayılı Resmî Gazete’de yayımlanan
6698 sayılı Kişisel Verilerin Korunması Kanunu (6698 sayılı Kanun) kapsamında
Kahramanmaraş Sütçü İmam Üniversitesi (Üniversite) olarak “Veri Sorumlusu” sıfatıyla 6698
sayılı Kanun’un “Veri Sorumlusunun Aydınlatma Yükümlülüğü” başlıklı 10 uncu maddesi
gereğince hazırlanmış olup bilgilendirme amaçlıdır.
       Üniversite olarak, Üniversite ile ilişkili tüm ilgili kişilere ait kişisel verilerin
güvenliğinin ve mahremiyetinin sağlanmasına, bu verilerin 6698 sayılı Kanun’a uygun olarak
işlenerek muhafaza edilmesine büyük önem vermekteyiz. 6698 sayılı Kanun kapsamında
“Veri Sorumlusu” sıfatıyla aydınlatma yükümlülüğü gereğince; veri sorumlusunun ve varsa
temsilcisinin kimliği, verilerin hangi amaçla işleneceği, işlenen verilerin kimlere ve hangi
amaçla aktarılabileceği, veri toplamanın yöntemi ve hukuki sebebi ile 6698 sayılı Kanunun 11
inci maddesinde sayılan diğer hakları konusunda ilgili kişileri aydınlatmak için bu
bilgilendirme metni hazırlanmıştır.
      1. Veri Sorumlusu
       6698 sayılı Kanun uyarınca, “Veri Sorumlusu” sıfatıyla Üniversite olarak, ilgili kişilerin
kişisel verilerini aşağıda açıklanan amaçlar kapsamında ve ilgili mevzuat tarafından belirlenen
sınırlar çerçevesinde işlemekteyiz.
      2. Kişisel Verilerin Hangi Amaçla İşleneceği
       Üniversite tarafından toplanan kişisel verileriniz, 6698 sayılı Kanun tarafından
öngörülen; hukuka ve dürüstlük kurallarına uygun olma, doğru ve gerektiğinde güncel olma,
belirli, açık ve meşru amaçlar için işlenme, işlendikleri amaçla bağlantılı, sınırlı ve ölçülü
olma, ilgili mevzuatta öngörülen veya işlendikleri amaç için gerekli olan süre kadar muhafaza
edilme ilkelerine uygun olarak ve 6698 sayılı Kanun’un 5 inci ve 6 ncı maddelerinde
belirtilen kişisel verilerin işlenme şartları çerçevesinde, aşağıda yer alan amaçlarla
işlenmektedir.

      3. İşlenen Verilerin Kimlere ve Hangi Amaçla Aktarılabileceği
       Kişisel verileriniz; “Veri Sorumlusu” sıfatına sahip Üniversitemiz tarafından eğitim,
sağlık, akademik ve idari faaliyetlerin yürütülmesi, sunulan hizmetlerden faydalanmanız için
gerekli çalışmaların ilgili iş birimleri tarafından yapılması, üniversitemizin ve üniversitemizle
iş ilişkisi içerisinde olan kişilerin hukuki ve ticari güvenliğinin temini, üniversite tesis ve
binalarının iç ve dış lokasyonlarında fiziksel güvenliğini ve denetiminin sağlanması, hukuki
uyum süreçlerinin yürütülmesi, mali işlerin yerine getirilmesi amaçlarıyla; Yükseköğretim
Kurulu, SGK gibi kurumlar, bakanlıklar, yargı mercileri gibi kanunen yetkili kılınmış kamu
kurum ve kuruluşlarına, mevzuatın izin verdiği hallerde işbirliği yaptığı/yapacağı, kuruluşlara,
taraflara, anlaşmalı kuruluşlara, ödeme sistemi kuruluşlarına, 6698 sayılı Kanun’un 8 inci ve 9
uncu maddelerinde belirtilen hususlar gereğince sınırlı olarak aktarılabilecektir.
       Üniversite tarafından kişisel verileriniz, 6698 sayılı Kanun’un 4(2) maddesindeki
öngörülen ilkeler ışığında; açık rıza temini suretiyle veya Kanun’un 5(2) ve 6(3)
maddelerinde öngörülen durumların varlığı halinde açık rıza temin edilmeksizin ve Kanunun
8 inci ve 9 uncu maddelerindeki kurallar uyarınca, Kişisel Verileri Koruma Kurulu tarafından
tespit edilecek yeterli korumaya sahip yabancı ülkeler ilan edildikten sonra sadece bu
ülkelerde yerleşik kişi ve kuruluşlara, yeterli korumanın bulunmadığı tespit ve ilan edilen
ülkeler için ise, Türkiye’deki ve ilgili yabancı ülkedeki veri sorumlularının yeterli bir
korumayı yazılı olarak taahhüt ettiği ve ilgili aktarım açısından Kişisel Verileri Koruma
Kurulu’nun izninin temin edilebildiği hallerle sınırlı olmak kaydıyla yurtdışına veri
aktarılabilecektir.

olarak kişisel verileriniz toplanabilecektir.

     5. Kişisel Veri Sahibinin 6698 sayılı Kanun’un 11 inci Maddesinde Sayılan Hakları
      Kişisel veri sahiplerinin haklarına ilişkin talepleri, aşağıda düzenlenen yöntemlerle
Üniversitemize iletilmesi durumunda, Üniversitemiz ilgili talebi en kısa sürede ve en geç otuz
gün içinde ücretsiz olarak sonuçlandıracaktır. Ancak, işlemin ayrıca bir maliyet gerektirmesi
halinde, Üniversitemiz tarafından Kişisel Verileri Koruma Kurulunca belirlenen tarifedeki
